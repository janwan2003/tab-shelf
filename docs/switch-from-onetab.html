Switch from OneTab to Tabhaven in 10 seconds: import your tabs with backups

OneTab can export your saved tabs as plain text. Tabhaven reads that text directly. Copy, paste, done, and from then on every change is backed up to files you can see.

Before you start: keep OneTab installed until you've checked that everything arrived in Tabhaven. Uninstalling a browser extension deletes the data it stored.

Step by step

  1. Export from OneTab

    Open the OneTab page and use its Export URLs option. OneTab shows all your saved tabs as text, one per line, in the form URL | title, for example:

    https://example.com/article | An article I meant to read
https://example.org/docs | Project documentation

  2. Copy the text

    Click in the text box, select everything with Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on a Mac) and copy it. If you prefer, save it as a .txt file instead. That also gives you a spare copy.

  3. Paste into Tabhaven's Import box

    Open Tabhaven's shelf page, open the Import box, and paste the text (or upload the .txt file). Your tabs appear on the shelf.

  4. Check, then relax

    Search for a few tabs you know you had. Tabhaven writes its backup files to Downloads/Tabhaven/ straight away, so your imported list already has a copy outside the browser.

Don't have Tabhaven yet? It is free and open source, and coming soon to the Firefox Add-ons store and the Chrome Web Store.

What you gain

  • Automatic backup files. A JSON file and a readable HTML page of links in Downloads/Tabhaven/, refreshed after every change, plus 7 daily rotating copies. They survive reinstalls, extension ID changes and browser resets, and Tabhaven can import them back.
  • Version history. Every delete, restore, import or edit takes a snapshot first, so you can undo it or roll the whole shelf back to an earlier point.
  • Saves that are confirmed before tabs close. Each save is a durable IndexedDB transaction (the browser's built-in database). Tabs are closed only after the write has been committed and read back. If anything fails, your tabs stay open.
  • Search over title and URL that stays responsive with 10,000+ saved tabs.
  • Exports in four formats: JSON, URL | title text, HTML or Markdown.

What stays familiar

One click on the toolbar button saves every tab in the window into a named group and closes the tabs to free memory. Restore one tab, a whole group, or a group into a new window with one click. Groups can be renamed, starred to stay at the top, or locked so that restoring them doesn't remove their tabs.

FAQ

I lost my saved tabs. Can I get them back?

Often, at least partly. In general:

  • Look for an existing export or backup file. Search your Downloads and Documents folders for .txt, .json or .html files from an earlier export.
  • Check your browser history. Press Ctrl+H to open your browser's history. On a Mac, it's Cmd+Y in Chrome and Cmd+Shift+H in Firefox. Pages you opened recently are listed there, and the browser's History menu has a list of recently closed tabs and windows.
  • Don't uninstall the extension while you're trying. Uninstalling deletes its storage.
  • From now on, keep automatic backups, so the next problem costs you nothing.

Does importing change anything in OneTab?

No. Tabhaven only reads the text you paste or the file you upload. It doesn't access OneTab or its data.

Can I import from other tab managers too?

Yes. Tabhaven reads JSON exports from Session Buddy, Tab Session Manager, Toby and most other tab managers (it finds the URLs in any JSON file), as well as a plain list of URLs.

Can I go back to OneTab later?

Tabhaven can export your shelf as URL | title text, the same line format OneTab's export uses. It can also export JSON, HTML and Markdown.

Is Tabhaven free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT license, with no account, no server and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

Not affiliated with OneTab

Tabhaven is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to OneTab or its developers. OneTab is named on this page only to explain how to move data from its export format.
