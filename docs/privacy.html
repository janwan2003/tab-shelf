Privacy policy

Tabhaven collects nothing

No data is collected, and nothing is transmitted. Your tabs stay on your computer.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No server. Tabhaven has nowhere to send your data, and it makes no network requests.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising.
  • Open source under the MIT license, so anyone can check these statements against the source code.

What Tabhaven stores, and where

To do its job, Tabhaven stores the titles and URLs of the tabs you save, your group names and settings (such as starred and locked groups), and snapshots for the version history. All of it is kept in two places, both on your computer:

  1. The extension's local storage inside your Chrome profile (IndexedDB and extension storage). Only Tabhaven can read it. Removing the extension deletes it.
  2. Backup files in Downloads/Tabhaven/: a JSON file, an HTML page of links, and 7 daily rotating copies. These are ordinary files that you control. You can open, move or delete them at any time.

If your Downloads folder is synced by a service such as Dropbox or OneDrive, the backup files will be synced by that service under its own terms. That's your choice; Tabhaven itself never uploads anything.

What is shared

Nothing. Tabhaven does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data with anyone, including the people who make it.

Permissions and why they're needed

Chrome permissions requested by Tabhaven and their purpose
PermissionWhy
tabsRead the titles and URLs of the tabs you save, close them after saving, and reopen them when you restore.
storageKeep settings in the extension's local storage.
unlimitedStorageLet a large shelf grow past the browser's default storage quota.
downloadsWrite backup files to Downloads/Tabhaven/.
downloads.uiWrite those backups quietly, without a download notification after every change.
alarmsRun the daily backup schedule.
contextMenusAdd Tabhaven options to the right-click menu.

Deleting your data

Delete groups or tabs on the shelf page, or remove the extension to delete everything in its local storage. To remove the backups as well, delete the Downloads/Tabhaven/ folder.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, third-party scripts or external fonts. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate the service; see GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Changes and questions

Any change to this policy will be published on this page and in the project's GitHub repository, where you can also ask questions by opening an issue.
